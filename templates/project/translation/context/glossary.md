# Working glossary

Make one copy inside each target-language directory. Replace every `TO DECIDE` before
translating and add terms as they appear. A term must not vary silently between chapters.

Policy values: `Preserve` (keep in the source form) or `Translate` (use the target form).

| Source term | Meaning or function | Target form | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TO ADD | TO ADD | TO DECIDE | Preserve / Translate |

## Names and places

Record every recurring person, nickname, place, institution, title, song and occupational
term before or during its first translated chapter.
